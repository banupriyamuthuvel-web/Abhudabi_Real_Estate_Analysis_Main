# Abu Dhabi Real Estate: One-Page Summary
*Illustrative analysis on synthetic sample data. *

**Audience:** property investors and buyers | **Period:** 2021 - Sep 2026 | **Scope:** 12 areas, ~41k sales, ~15k rental contracts

## Question
Which areas offer the best value, how have prices moved, and when should one transact?

## Headline results
| Metric | Result |
|---|---|
| Market average price (2025) | ~AED 12,700 per sqm |
| Price growth 2021-2025 | ~+42% (8-12% per year) |
| Highest / lowest price area | Al Maryah (~19.5k) / Al Ghadeer (~6.2k) per sqm |
| Highest / lowest gross yield (2025) | Al Ghadeer (~8.7%) / Al Maryah (~5.2%) |
| Peak / trough months | Dec (index 129) / Jul (index 73) |

## What it means
1. **Yield and prestige trade off.** Suburban areas deliver the highest rental income per dirham invested; island areas deliver lower yield and rely on capital value.
2. **Growth did not separate winners much.** Areas grew at similar rates, so yield is the main differentiator.
3. **Timing matters.** Summer is quiet; Q4/Q1 is busy.

## Recommendations
- Income investors: shortlist Al Ghadeer, MBZ City, Al Reef, Khalifa City; check service charges and vacancy.
- Prestige buyers: Saadiyat, Yas, Al Maryah, accepting lower yield.
- Buyers: negotiate in Jul-Aug.

## Caveats
Synthetic data; mix effects (apartments vs villas) influence area averages; gross yield excludes costs.
